A note on where the framework idea came from

An earlier downloaded research document proposed a 20-issue "operational governance framework" for AMS, complete with a RACI matrix naming specific internal roles and a test-suite figure ("1,520 tests") that turned out to be copied verbatim from a separate, earlier fabricated document — two "independent" sources landing on an identical invented number is evidence of copied origin, not corroboration. That document's specific claims (named facilities, a claimed Project Kuiper/Amazon Robotics/AWS-data-center client relationship) were checked against the real job posting and found unsupported. This page keeps the parts of that idea that are actually generic, well-established cost-engineering practice — the pillar taxonomy, the should-cost/ MHR/variance mechanics — and drops everything presented as fact about AMS specifically that isn't.

Built as a purpose-specific companion to the AMS fit brief, narrative work sample, variance walkthrough, and 90-day plan — not a replacement for any of them. Zero external dependencies; every calculation on this page runs client-side in the functions below.
